# Vision

**Purpose:** a peaceful, distraction-free place to write rough drafts. It is for getting the words out, not for editing or perfecting. When a draft is done, it moves to another tool for editing.

**Who it is for:** one writer, its author, running it on their own machine. Others are welcome to set it up for themselves, but that is not the goal.

**Where it is headed:**
- It keeps running on a home machine, reached privately (for example, over Tailscale) from a phone and other devices.
- It may become a native app someday.
- Getting drafts out of Draft becomes easier than copy and paste, with Scrivener first in line for when fiction writing resumes.

**Guiding principles:**
1. Rough drafts only: no editing features, no polishing tools.
2. Peaceful and uncluttered: every screen earns its place.
3. No gimmicks: no gamification, rewards, tricks, or streak or day counting.
4. The words are safe: saving, backups and upgrades never risk the writing.
5. Local and private: no accounts, no cloud service, and nothing leaves the machine unless the writer sends it.

**Non-goals:** editing and revision tools, collaboration, hosting for others, AI writing help, and any motivation or tracking features.
